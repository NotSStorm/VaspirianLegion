IMPERIAL ORDERS & HONORS
------------------------
• AHLGRIM'S CIRCLE
  Andour's Medal of Honor, given for acts of great courage in the defense of the 
  country. Only given by official members of the Imperial family of Andour.

• IMPERIAL ORDER OF AHLGRIM
  Awarded personally by the Emperor or Crown Prince of Andour for the achievements 
  of only the most well esteemed and notable individuals in the history of the Empire.

• ORDER OF THE ANDOURAN EMPIRE
  Awarded by the Sovereignty of Andour to the most notable members of the Empire in 
  commemoration of their efforts and contributions to the nation and their legions.
  - Classes: Knight, Commander, Grand Cross

• ORDER OF THE ARCHITECTS
  Awarded to the most respective and highest contributing developers and artists 
  of Andour. Only the most exceptional artists and the most committed developers 
  of the nation are able to earn this.

• ORDER OF THE CITADEL
  Awarded to the most notable staff members of the Empire for outstanding 
  performance, extreme loyalty, and unyielding commitment to Andour and its cause.
  - Classes: Knight, Commander

• ORDER OF THE GOLD GRIFFIN
  Given for undying loyalty, combat expertise, and carrying oneself in a manner 
  befitting of the values of Andour. To become a member of the Order of the Gold 
  Griffin, one must be inducted as a squire under an existing knight.
  - Ranks: Esquire, Knight, Baron, Earl, Duke, Prince


SHIELDS
-------
• GOLD SHIELD (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Given for high level command ability in leading multiple regiments and formations, 
  this medal is only available to regimental commanders+, but is typically awarded 
  to general staff. In order to receive an upgrade in class, one must lead a 
  larger-scale formation (i.e. elevating from a brigade to a corps or fielding the army).

• SILVER SHIELD (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Given for tactical ability in leading groups of rankers and for outstanding 
  leadership initiative, only applicable to capable commanders on the field of 
  battle. In order to receive an upgrade in class, one must have objectively 
  improved as a line commander and shall be reviewed and confirmed by the respective 
  medal committee.

• BRONZE SHIELD (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Given for excellence in administration, training, and organization of a military 
  formation, applicable to any staff member. In order to receive an upgrade in 
  class, one must have done significantly more work for their respective regiment.


REGIONAL AWARDS
---------------
• EMBODIMENT OF VASPIRIA
  Awarded for consistent service, dedicated loyalty, exceptional valor, and 
  steadfast integrity in service to Vaspira.


CORPS MEDALS
------------
• GUARD KNIGHT COMMENDATION
  Awarded to the most esteemed and notable commanders of the Guard, these individuals 
  are closely associated with the success and legacy of the Guard. Truly only the 
  absolute best earn this medal.

• IMPERIAL GUARD CROSS (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
  Reserved strictly for Guardsmen, this medal is awarded for displaying deep loyalty 
  and dedication to the Guard as well as exemplary valor on the field of battle.

• ORDER OF THE WHITE TIGER
  Kaito's Medal of Honor, given for acts of great courage and dedication in the 
  defense and in service of the dominion.

• PROVINCIAL ARMY CROSS (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
  Awarded only to members of the Provincial Army who show exemplary dedication, 
  martial prowess, and valiant actions on and off the field of battle in service 
  to their regiments and greater nation as a whole.


REGIMENTAL AWARDS & ACCOLADES
-----------------------------
• PARAGON OF MIGHT & HONOR
  Given out at each ceremony to commemorate the top members of the regiments. Only 
  three people per regiment can be awarded this at a time. An extra medal can be 
  awarded for every additional line a regiment rallies.

• IMPERIAL MERIT
  Awarded to long standing veterans of the army who remain consistently active and 
  committed, recipients of this award are recognized as foundational pillars of 
  their respective legions.

• CROSS OF CONSUMMATE VALOR
  A distinctive award earned through top fragging a victorious Andouran brigade-wide 
  or larger battle. Awarded on the spot and re-announced at the next ceremony.

• ARTISANS ACCOLADE
  A precursor to the Order of the Architects, given out for generalized propaganda 
  efforts for one's respective regiment or corps (clips, pictures, VODs, artwork).

• SILVER ARCHITECT
  Awarded for prominent architectural, design, and development contributions to the nation.

• CANNONEERS CROSS
  An exclusive commemoration awarded to members of artillery units within the Andouran 
  Empire who have displayed immense dedication, valor, and skill operating batteries.

• RECRUITMENT CROSS (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Awarded to Andourans who’ve gone above and beyond to expand their ranks.
  - 3rd Class: 15 recruits
  - 2nd Class: 30 recruits
  - 1st Class: 50 recruits

• STAR OF SOLIDARITY
  Awarded to those who do their part in keeping their community active, strong, and 
  holding high morale.

• COLOR GUARD COMMENDATION
  Earned by those who give their all into consistently and successfully defending 
  Andouran colors on the field.

• VALOR IN DEATH (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Given for exemplary combat skill and acts of heroism, typically reserved for 
  regimental specialists.

• HONORARY SERVICE (1st Class, 2nd Class, 3rd Class)
  Dedicated to those who’ve shown immense dedication, loyalty, and activity to 
  their regiments and nation.
